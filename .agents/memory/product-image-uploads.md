---
name: Product image uploads
description: The user's standing expectations for adding product images to the Dermi Natural Healthcare catalog.
---

For this website, review every newly uploaded product image and attach it to the matching catalog product without replacing existing product information or images. The user expects to upload more images over time.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested careful matching, no duplicate products, preservation of existing content, and a scalable path for future uploads.

**How to apply:** inspect the image and existing product records first; append its path and descriptive alt text to the matching product's image list. Add a product record only when the upload clearly represents a distinct product absent from the catalog. Keep original aspect ratios and use the existing gallery.
