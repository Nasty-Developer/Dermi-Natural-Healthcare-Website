name: Product image uploads
description: The user's standing expectations for adding product images to the Dermi Natural Healthcare catalog.
---

For this website, review every newly uploaded product image and follow the user's product mapping. Similar brand or product names alone do not prove that an upload belongs to an existing listing; the user may identify it as a distinct new product. The user expects to upload more images over time.

**Why:** the user clarified that a batch of uploads represented new products even though their labels resembled existing catalog items.

**How to apply:** inspect each image and group multiple views of one item into a single gallery. When the user confirms items are new, create separate product records and keep existing product data and original images intact. Do not merge solely by name similarity.
