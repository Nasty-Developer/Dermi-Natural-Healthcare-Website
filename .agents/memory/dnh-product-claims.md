---
name: Product content accuracy
description: Dermi Natural's fact and claims boundary for product catalog content.
---

Use only product details supplied in the official update brief or clearly visible on official packaging. Do not add unverified benefits, medical or treatment claims, ingredients, or efficacy promises.

**Why:** The imported Dermi Natural project specifies factual product copy and prohibits inventing product claims or package details.

**How to apply:** Verify catalog and detail-page copy against the brief or packaging before editing. If a statement is uncertain, omit it rather than infer it.
